R28.4.38 EQ convolution default rule

Default per-instrument EQ pole count = clamp(130 - active_instrument_count, 2, 128), with active instrument count clamped to 2..128.

Examples:
- 2 instruments -> 128 EQ poles per instrument
- 3 instruments -> 127 EQ poles per instrument
- 48 instruments -> 82 EQ poles per instrument
- 128 instruments -> 2 EQ poles per instrument

This is the default topology only. Saved custom EQ topologies remain intact; users can add/remove poles up to 128 per instrument. Default poles are linearly distributed in Hz from 10 Hz to 24 kHz, with equal frequency increments for a given pole count. Legacy 8-point shape curves are interpolated across the generated topology. No stochastic/probability behavior is involved.
