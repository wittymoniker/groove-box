RUNTIME-AUDIO-MIC-BROWSER-MICROSD
Date: 2026-10-03

R23k is a runtime-fix release built on the R23j1 verifier-synchronized lineage.

FIXES
- Microphone live level: resolves the selected input by stable device name in the GUI process rather than reusing a PortAudio integer obtained in the isolated device-probe process.
- Microphone continuity: level is updated directly from the sounddevice callback; stale/inactive streams are restarted instead of remaining nominally open with a frozen meter.
- Physical playback: ranks real ALSA playback endpoints, strongly prefers the same physical card used by hardware volume, penalizes HDMI/digital-only endpoints for laptop-speaker playback, and proves that aplay remains open before attaching FFmpeg.
- Web Browser tab: first navigation is deferred until after the tab can paint, and the QWebEngineView/profile/renderer is retained when leaving the tab rather than being stopped/evicted and recreated.
- microSD reader rescan: removes reader-only automatic rescans, bounds explicit reader discovery to four short attempts with one controlled Realtek rebind, and always returns the Rescan Reader button/UI to a completed state.

PRESERVED
- Dell Latitude 3510 HDA/ALC3204 first audio policy and linked hardware/system volume/media keys.
- R23j universal FFmpeg -> 48 kHz stereo PCM -> physical ALSA fallback.
- R23i Realtek SD reader support, removable storage UI, Install-to-SSD, camera/media, Performance, and prior sOS lineage.
- Project/export sample rates remain unchanged; only physical playback is adapted to a compatible hardware boundary.
