Groovebox v35.22ze — BOOK ISX/ISY CANONICAL — 2026-09-23

This revision makes the author's written summation series authoritative for the
Trig Math Engine forward route across Python, Rust, C++, Julia, sCode, OT
helpers, script environments, audio helpers, and generated-game math.

Canonical forward contract:
  isn(gamma) = gamma^2/2! - gamma^4/4! + gamma^6/6! - ...
  isx(gamma) = 1 - isn(gamma)
  isy(gamma) = 1 - isn(gamma - pi/2)
  cos(gamma) = isx(gamma)
  sin(gamma) = isy(gamma)
  tan(gamma) = isy(gamma) / isx(gamma)

The old half-angle forward shortcut 2*func(x/2), including sin(x)=isn(2x)/2,
is not used by the Trig Math Engine ON path. The inverse-isosceles coordinate is
kept separate and is evaluated with its corresponding Maclaurin/binomial series.
