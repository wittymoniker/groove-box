SEQUENCE SCROLL + AUTOMATION GROWTH FIX — 2026-09-11

- Sequence Length remains user-resizable from 1 through 1024.
- A manual length edit now receives its own user length lock, preventing canonical
  bank refreshes from snapping a manually chosen value back to a seed-derived
  length (the observed value was commonly 24).
- Step and Automation strips now use explicit content geometry. Cells stretch
  equally to fill the viewport when they fit; when their readable minimum width
  exceeds the viewport, a real horizontal scrollbar is exposed.
- The Automation viewport reserves enough height for its horizontal scrollbar.
- Growing Automation creates each missing new cell as a persisted ON point.
- New Automation points inherit the selected/nearest point's Operator, Sequence,
  Offset, Morph, envelope and synth-parameter state.
- Attack, Release, selected synth parameter and value are persisted on the
  individual Automation point and consumed by automation resolution.
- Fresh synchronized Automation cells are materialized with complete stored state.
