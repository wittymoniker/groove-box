2026-09-30

R16 adds a target-hardware acceptance test rather than treating enumeration as proof of working audio.

Performance -> Hardware -> Physical Audio I/O Test:
- rejects Dummy/Null outputs and monitor/loopback inputs;
- chooses a real PortAudio/PipeWire output + microphone;
- validates a 48 kHz full-duplex stream;
- plays a short 997 Hz test tone at modest level;
- records the microphone simultaneously;
- measures microphone RMS and peak;
- measures captured 997 Hz energy relative to nearby room-noise spectrum;
- PASS requires actual acoustic/electrical return of the emitted tone.

The test runs on a worker pool, never the Qt GUI thread. It does not change canonical state.
It is also available at /usr/bin/sos-audio-io-test in sOS.

No claim is made that the Dell Latitude hardware has already physically passed inside the build environment; that proof is intentionally generated on the booted target by this test.
